# Recordings

Put your voice recording here (any format: `.m4a` from Voice Memos, `.mp3`, `.wav`, ...),
then run `/clone-voice` in Claude Code.

**Aim for 30-60 seconds**, in a quiet room, speaking the way you want your podcast to sound.
If you don't know what to say, read this passage aloud (about 45 seconds):

> I've always liked the idea that you can learn almost anything if you break it into small
> enough pieces. When I started this podcast, I didn't have a studio, a plan, or much of an
> audience. I had a laptop, a cup of coffee, and a long list of questions I wanted to explore.
> Some episodes came together quickly. Others took weeks, because the answer kept changing the
> more I read. What surprised me most was how often the best ideas came from conversations,
> not books. So that's what this show is really about: slowing down, asking better questions,
> and sharing what I find along the way. Thanks for being here. Let's get started.
