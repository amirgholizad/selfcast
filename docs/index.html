Selfcast

Turn any text into a podcast episode in your own voice.

This is a synthetic voice: the author's own voice, cloned from about a minute of recording and generated on a MacBook Air M4. Nothing was edited by hand.

Transcript

Hi! What you're hearing right now isn't a recording. (chuckles) Well, not exactly.

It's a clone of my voice, made from about one minute of me talking, and generated entirely on my laptop. No cloud, no uploads, no subscriptions.

I wrote a script, ran one command, and a couple of minutes later, I had this. And if a sentence sounds off, I can redo just that line, without touching the rest.

So if you've ever wanted to start a podcast, but never found the time to record one, give Selfcast a try. Everything you need is in this repo.
