# The Two-Minute Rule

<!-- An example script. Notes like this are never read aloud. See the README for the full syntax. -->

Welcome to the very first episode. I'm really glad you're here.

Today I want to share one small idea that changed how I get things done. It's called the two-minute rule, and honestly, it's almost too simple. [chuckle]

Here's how it works:
- If a task takes less than two minutes, do it right now.
- If it takes longer, shrink it until the first step fits into two minutes.

Want to read more books? Don't plan to read a whole chapter. Just open the book and read one page. Want to start running? Put on your running shoes and step outside. That's it.

[pause 1s]

So why does this work? Because the hardest part of almost anything is starting. Once you've begun, momentum takes over. Two minutes turns into ten, and ten turns into an hour, more often than you'd expect.

And even on the days when it doesn't, you've still kept a small promise to yourself. Over time, those small promises add up. You become the kind of person who shows up.

Here's your challenge for this week. Pick one thing you've been putting off, and give it just two minutes today.
